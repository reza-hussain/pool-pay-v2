# Refunds are computed from each Member's own remaining balance, not pro-rata

Supersedes the refund-formula portion of [ADR-0006](0006-pool-closure-and-refund.md) (pro-rata by total contribution); ADR-0006's no-auto-expiry Closure-trigger rule is unaffected. Amends [ADR-0009](0009-majority-vote-emergency-refund.md) by inheritance — the emergency majority vote reuses the Closure mechanism, so it now pays out under this same formula with no change to the vote/trigger logic itself.

ADR-0006's pro-rata rule assumed contribution size was the only thing that mattered at refund time, splitting *leftover* balance proportionally to total deposits. That breaks once spend cost is attributed per-Member ([ADR-0019](0019-equal-split-spend-mechanics.md)): if Members deposit and spend unevenly, pro-rata refunds the leftover in the wrong proportion — a careful spender ends up subsidizing one who wasn't, even though each Member's own remaining balance already tracks their true individual position throughout.

We now refund each Member exactly **their own remaining balance** — total Deposits minus their attributed share of every spend recorded while they were active — at every point money is returned: ordinary end-of-trip Closure, a Member's own departure, and emergency vote-Closure alike. All three now use the identical formula; none is a special case.

This also resolves a fairness gap in how departures were handled: previously, a Member removed mid-Pool wasn't refunded until Closure, at which point pro-rata paid them a share of the *whole Pool's* leftover balance rather than their own. With per-Member balances tracked continuously, a departure can be refunded correctly at the moment it happens, and no longer needs Organizer-only gating for fairness reasons — **a Member may now remove themselves ("self-leave")**, since the math produces the same fair result regardless of who triggers the departure.

**The manual-override valve:** the default computed refund can still be wrong for a specific departure, because "did this Member actually benefit from a given spend" isn't always the same as "were they active when it was recorded." Example: fuel bought for a leg of the trip a Member ends up not attending, because they left immediately afterward — active when it was recorded, but no benefit received. The app can't detect this automatically (the same benefit-vs-activity gap noted in ADR-0018), so the Organizer (or the group) can manually adjust one Member's computed refund at the point of departure. Unlike a rare dispute-resolution tool, this review is meant to happen as a normal, lightweight step on **every** mid-trip departure, not only when someone objects — the judgment call it resolves is common, not rare.
